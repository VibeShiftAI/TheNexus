### A4. The strategic owner-income target (kept separate)

**Target:** $20,000/month in owner income, after business expenses and before personal taxes. [README 13; Plan 5]

This target is **not** a 90-day acceptance criterion and not a revenue forecast. [README 15; Plan 16] Passing the 90-day outcome does not mean the target is met, and missing the target in 90 days does not mean the launch failed.

- **Illustrative mature month, to prove later:** two $10,000 pilots + four $2,000 support agreements + one $2,000 assessment = $30,000 earned fees and 124 founder delivery hours (2×50 + 4×4 + 8), leaving 36 hours of a 160-hour month for sales, admin and contingency. Less about $6,000 expenses and $4,000 retained, that leaves about $20,000 for owner pay. This is a capacity test, not a demand forecast or the starting workload. [Plan 138–140]
- **When the target counts as met:** only when collections, delivery capacity, reserves, taxes, benefits and forward demand support it **for several months in a row**, judged by trailing three-month earned revenue, cash receipts, owner-pay capacity, booked work, support burden and client concentration (months 4–6 review). [Plan 117, 144]
- **At verified launch completion:** Praxis proposes the next growth goal for Robert to adopt. It does not replace the current goal automatically. [README 15; Handoff 24]

## Part B: One-page offer sheet (internal; price hypotheses)

**For:** owners and operations leaders at small professional-services firms (about 5–50 staff) with a recurring research, reporting or admin workflow that eats staff time. **Not for:** automating consequential clinical, legal, hiring or financial decisions.

**Start here: free 30-minute fit call.** Purpose: qualify (problem frequency, current labor and rework, cost of mistakes, decision authority, data access, budget) and agree a next step. Solution design is reserved for the paid assessment. [Plan 34, 92]

| | **1. Workflow assessment** | **2. Implementation pilot** | **3. Care and improvement** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price hypothesis** | $2,000 fixed | $10,000 fixed, offered after an assessment | $2,000/month |
| **Scope** | One week. Up to two stakeholder interviews, a map of one workflow, baseline measures, data/access review, one written implementation proposal. | One workflow over 2–4 weeks. Up to two agreed integrations, agreed acceptance checks, documentation, user training, handover. | Monthly outcome review, agreed maintenance and small improvements. One-business-day response target; resolution time depends on the issue. |
| **Founder budget** | Up to 8 hours | Up to 50 hours including QA. Quote only when the scope fits. | Up to 4 hours/month |
| **Client owns** | The workflow map, baseline and proposal delivered to them; their own data and systems access | The commissioned deliverables, per the agreement. Production model/API and hosting accounts are client-owned and billed to the client directly where practical. | Their accounts and environments; support logs for their workflow |
| **VibeShift keeps** | Its reusable methods, templates and tools | Its reusable tools and Praxis, kept distinct from the commissioned work | Same |
| **Done (acceptance boundary)** | Assessment is complete when the proposal and baseline are delivered and walked through with the client. Approving or buying the pilot is a separate decision. | Accepted when the **acceptance checks agreed in the SOW before kickoff** pass and the client signs off. Anything outside the two integrations or the named workflow is a change order. | Each month is complete when the review is held and agreed items are done or carried forward. Work beyond 4 hours or outside agreed maintenance needs a separate scope. |
| **Proposed payment (for attorney review)** | Paid in full before kickoff | 50% before kickoff, 25% at the agreed demonstration, 25% at acceptance | Paid monthly in advance |

[Plan 26–34, 42, 75]

**Applies to every offer (proposed, for the attorney to set):** written acceptance criteria, revision limits, payment dates, handling of client delays, cancellation and change orders; confidentiality and client-data permissions; disclosure of AI/cloud subcontractors. [Plan 32, 42] The exact IP split, revision count and liability terms are **not settled** and are not stated here as terms.

**Measuring results:** each pilot records time per task, error/rework rate, adoption and running cost before and after, with the sample size and limits. Client approval is recorded separately from technical QA. Testimonial and case-study permission is requested only after acceptance. [Plan 94]

**How we will test the price hypotheses:** for every qualified conversation, record whether the price was accepted, negotiated or refused, and the objection given. For every engagement, compare actual founder hours with the budget above (8 / 50 / 4). If hours run over, change scope, price or delivery resources before taking more concurrent work. Robert decides any price change. [Plan 24, 92, 138]
